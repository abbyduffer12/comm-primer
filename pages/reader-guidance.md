# What the reader should do

AI can be a great tool for helping us communicate, but how do we make sure it is not changing what we actually want to say? 

Throughout this website, we have explored how AI can influence the way our messages are written, delivered, and interpreted. Now it's time to think about what we can do to stay in control of our communication. 

## 1. Know What you are trying to communicate

Before asking AI to rewrite something, think about your original intention. Are you apologizing to a friend? Asking a coworker for help? Trying to sound more professional?

Having a clear intention makes it easier to recognize when AI changes your message in a way you didn't want. 

## 2. Make sure you message still sounds like you 

Using an example of writing a friend an apology. AI might turn a heartfelt message into something that sounds more formal and robotic rather than emotional and intentional. Even if the grammar is perfect, your friend might question whether the words actually reflect your feelings 

This is where Shannon and Weaver's model and semiotics becomes useful. A message can be delivered successfully, but the words and tone can still influence how the receiver understands it. 

Before sending an emotional message read it back to yourself and ask, "Would I actually say this?" 

## 3. Think about who is receiving the message 

The way you communicate with close friends is probably different from how you communicate with you boss or even a coworker. 

Remember Stuart Hall's idea of encoding and decoding. You might intend for a message to sound friendly, but the person receiving it could interpret it differently.

For example, when asking a coworker for help, AI might make your message sound more professional than necessary. That isn't always a bad thing, but it might not match the relationship you have with that person.

## 4. Use AI as an assistant, not your replacement 

AI can help with grammar, organization, and finding the right words. However, it shouldn't automatically make every decision about how you communicate.5. Choose an action and justify it with theory.

The Fourth Intention reminds us that AI can influence the words and tone of a message, even when the original idea comes from us.

Before clicking send, take a moment to review what AI created. Change anything that doesn't sound natural or accurately reflect what you mean.

## The Final Takeaway 

So, who is really speaking when AI helps write our messages? 

I believe the human is still the one speaking because we create the original intention and decide what gets sent. However, AI can become a co-author by influencing that intention is expressed. 

## AI can help you write a better message, but you should be the one deciding what that message says.
