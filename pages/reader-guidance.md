# What the reader should do

This page translates theory into practical steps for personal and professional communication decisions.

Suggested structure for your future content:

1. Identify the communication problem.
2. Diagnose it with at least two theory lenses.
3. Compare what each lens reveals and hides.
4. Choose an action and justify it with theory.

Use this page to present actionable recommendations your audience can apply immediately.
