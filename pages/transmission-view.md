# The transmission view and its limits

This page examines how the Shannon-Weaver model clarifies communication as signal transmission, noise management, and channel efficiency. It offers a strong framework for diagnosing technical message breakdowns.

It also introduces key limits of this model for human communication. Humanist critiques point out that people do not only send and receive signals; they interpret, resist, and reshape meaning through social context.
