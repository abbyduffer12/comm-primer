# Interpretation and intention

This page starts from Umberto Eco's framework of intentions and extends it with **intentio algorithmi** to name algorithmic intention effects not fully captured by classical models.

The goal is to compare authorial intent, textual affordances, reader interpretation, and platform-level computational shaping in one framework.

Use this page to draft your own argument about where intention resides when AI systems mediate communication.
