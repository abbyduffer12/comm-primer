# Interpretation and power

### Have you ever sent a message that someone completely misunderstood? 

You might send a text thinking it sounds friendly, but the person receiving it thinks you're upset. Even though you know what you meant, the other person might understand it differently.

This is exactly what communication theorist Stuart Hall explored through his encoding and decoding theory.

### What is encoding and decoding? 

Stuart Hall explained that people create messages with an intended meaning, but the person receiving the message doesn't always interpret it the same way.

**Encoding** is when you take your thoughts and intentions and turn them into a message.

**Decoding** is when someone receives that message and interprets what it means.

For example, you might text a friend "Okay." You intended it as a simple response, but your friend might think you're annoyed with them.

The message was received, but the meaning wasn't necessarily understood the way you intended.
