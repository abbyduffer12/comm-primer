# Meaning and culture

## Have you ever sent a message that someone took the wrong way? 

Sometimes, what we say and what someone understands are two completely different things. The words we choose, the tone we use, and the relationship with the person receiving the essage can influence how our words are interpreted. 

This is where (*semiotics*) come into play. 

## What is semiotics? 

Semiotics is the study of signs and symbols and how they create meaning. Think about NIKES famous phrase, "Just Do It." When you see those words paired with the NIKE swoosh, you probably recognize the brand quickly. This is because signs and symbols corry meaning we have learned to associate with them. 

The same thing happens in everyday conversation. Words, emojis, punctuation, and even the way someone normally text can influence how we interpret their message. 

## WHat happens when AI changes our words? 

the example apologizing to a friend. 

### My original apology: 
"I'm really sorry about what happened. I know I messed up, and you know I would never do anything to try and hurt your feelings."

### An example of an AI-rewritten apology: 
"I sincerely apologize for my actions and any emotional distress they may have caused."

Both messages communicate an apology, but do they feel the same? 

The original sounds more personal and emotional, while the rewritten version sounds formal and distant. Even though AI may have improved the wording, it also changed the way the apology could be interpreted. 

## Why does this matter? 

Imagine receiving that formal apology from your best friend. Would you think they were being sincere, or would you wonder why they sounded different from usual. 

From a semiotic perspective, the words and tone are signs that help us understand the meaning behind a message. When AI changes those signs, it can also influence how someone interprets the message. 

However, not everyone will react the same way. One person might appreciate the more thoughtful wording, while another might find it unnatural. 

(*The words we choose matter because meaning comes from more than just what a message says. It also comes from how the message is expressed and understood.*) 

### What should you remember? 

Before sending an AI-assisted message, think about the relationship you have the person receiving it. 

Ask yourself whether the wording, tone, and overall message still reflect what you want that person to understand. 

AI can help us find the right words, but we still need to consider what those words mean to the people reading them.
